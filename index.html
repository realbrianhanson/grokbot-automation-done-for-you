FAQ

What is a Grokbot?+

A persistent AI teammate with its own cloud computer. It signs into your tools, browses the web, and keeps working after you close your laptop.

Do I need a Grok subscription?+

Yes. Grokbot is included in Premium ($30/mo), Super ($100/mo), and Ultra ($200/mo) plans from SpaceXSI. That's billed separately from our setup fee.

Can I customize the workflows later?+

Absolutely. Small tweaks are included in your support window, and our monthly care plans cover ongoing changes and improvements.

What if it stops working?+

Our care plans include monitoring and uptime checks. If something breaks, we fix it. Without a plan, you can always reach out for a one-off repair.
